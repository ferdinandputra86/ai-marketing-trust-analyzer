# Experiment 01: Overhyped AI Copy

**Note:** The example below is fictional and for learning purposes only.

## Raw AI-style copy (example)

> Revolutionary AI-powered platform transforming recruitment forever with cutting-edge automation and seamless hiring experiences for modern teams.

## Why it feels generic

- **Hype stack:** Words like *revolutionary*, *cutting-edge*, and *seamless* add energy but little information.
- **No concrete outcome:** *Transforming recruitment forever* does not say what changes tomorrow.
- **Vague audience:** *Modern teams* could mean almost any company.
- **Product-category language:** *Platform* and *experiences* describe category, not value.

## Human-edited rewrite (example)

> Help hiring teams screen applicants faster by flagging role-fit signals early — so recruiters spend less time on manual resume review.

## What improved

| Signal | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Audience | Vague | Hiring teams / recruiters |
| Outcome | Abstract transformation | Faster screening, less manual work |
| Hype | High | Low |
| Verifiability | Unsupported superlatives | No fake stats added |

## Takeaway

AI can draft momentum quickly. A human editor should trade some excitement for **specificity** and **believable outcomes**.
