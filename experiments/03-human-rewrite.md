# Experiment 03: AI Draft vs Human Rewrite

**Note:** Examples are illustrative, not from a real product.

## Raw AI copy (example)

> Unlock the ultimate AI recruitment solution. Supercharge your hiring workflow with powerful automation and world-class results.

## Human-edited copy (example)

> Screen more qualified candidates in less time — built for in-house recruiters who are tired of manually reviewing every resume.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Dimension | AI draft | Human rewrite |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Specificity | Low (solution, workflow) | Higher (screen candidates, less time) |
| Audience | Missing | In-house recruiters |
| Hype | High (ultimate, supercharge, world-class) | Low |
| Logic | Implies universal great results | Realistic scope |
| Trust feel | Salesy | Grounded |

## Why the human rewrite improves trust

1. **Names the reader** — recruiters see themselves in the message.
2. **States a believable job-to-be-done** — less manual resume review.
3. **Avoids fake precision** — no invented stats or guarantees.
4. **Sounds like a person wrote it** — plain language beats buzzword density.

## Practical workflow

1. Use AI for a fast first draft.
2. Run a trust checklist (specificity, verifiability, clarity, audience, hype, logic).
3. Edit for proof, tone, and channel fit.
4. Verify any factual claim before publishing.

## Takeaway

AI is a strong **drafting** partner. Humans are still responsible for **judgment, verification, and voice**.
