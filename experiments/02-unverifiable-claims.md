# Experiment 02: Unverifiable Claims

**Note:** All numbers and authority claims below are fictional examples.

## Raw copy with risky claims (example)

> Our industry-leading AI is proven to deliver 3x faster hiring and guaranteed results for every company.

## Why unsupported claims are risky

- **Percentages and multiples** imply measurement. Without a source, readers may assume the number is invented.
- **"Proven" and "guaranteed"** raise the burden of proof.
- **"Industry-leading" and "every company"** are broad authority claims that are hard to defend.
- In regulated or reputation-sensitive categories, exaggerated claims can create brand and compliance risk.

## Rewrite without unsupported numbers (example)

> Teams use our tool to speed up first-pass resume screening — publish your own time-saved story when you have real customer data.

## What changed

1. Removed fictional performance statistics.
2. Replaced absolute guarantees with realistic framing.
3. Shifted proof responsibility to **documented customer outcomes** instead of invented benchmarks.

## Takeaway

If you do not have a source, do not ship the number. Marketing trust grows when claims are **defensible**, not just confident.
